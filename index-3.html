    Luigi Di Caro

    Associate Professor of Computer Science, University of Turin
    Natural language processingKnowledge representationHuman–AI interactionAI & law

    He leads natural language processing and legal informatics in the Social Computing group of the Department of Computer Science. His work spans NLP, machine learning, data mining and knowledge representation, with links to cognitive science, human–computer interaction and law.

    PhD in Computer Science (Turin, 2011, jointly supervised with Arizona State University). National Scientific Qualification as Full Professor in Computer Science (2025).
